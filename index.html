How we work

A Florida company doing the unglamorous part

HZA Chor LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations.

We handle administration and coordination. We are not a licensed contractor, a law firm or an accountancy practice: licensed trade work, legal questions and tax matters go to the appropriate professional, and we'll say so rather than guess.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

What exactly do you take on?

Whatever is agreed in the written scope — commonly intake, quotes, scheduling, confirmations, document preparation and project coordination. Decisions stay with you.

Do you do licensed trade work?

No. We coordinate licensed trades and suppliers; we don't perform work requiring a trade licence.

How does handover work?

Usually within a week: we map how things run now, set up the routes in, and take over on an agreed date. Nothing goes dark in between.

What does it cost?

It depends on volume and scope. Everything is quoted in writing after a free consultation, and nothing is charged before you accept.

Will customers know it's not us?

We work under your business name on correspondence where you want that, and we'll agree exactly how we identify ourselves before anything goes out.

Can we start small?

Yes, and it's usually sensible. Take one thing that keeps slipping and expand only if it's working.

A free consultation on what keeps slipping and what we could take on.
